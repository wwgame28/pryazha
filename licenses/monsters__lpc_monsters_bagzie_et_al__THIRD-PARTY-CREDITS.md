# Third-party credits & licences

All sprite art in this project comes from the **Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC)** community and related
OpenGameArt submissions. It is *not* original work by this project — the value this project adds is
the cataloguing, the direction/frame geometry, the renderer and the editor UI.

> **Every pack below must keep its attribution wherever its art is redistributed.**
> LPC art is largely CC-BY-SA / GPL / OGA-BY — i.e. share-alike. If your game uses these sheets,
> credit the authors listed here (the app has a Credits panel that shows the same list, and
> `catalog.json → packs` carries the machine-readable version).
